The Journey

How it happened, step by step.

1
The Problem

Two non-technical founders with a big vision

Both Dukancart founders had backgrounds in retail and logistics. They understood the customer deeply but had no idea how to build an app. Every developer they spoke to quoted 6–12 months.

2
Discovery

Applume's grocery product was everything — instantly

Within one call, the founders saw Applume's Grocery platform demo. Customer app, dark store management, delivery tracking, and admin panel — it was exactly what they'd imagined. They signed up the same day.

3
Build

Brand, stores, delivery zones configured in 7 days

Applume applied Dukancart's brand, configured delivery zones for City 1, onboarded their first 5 store partners, set up the delivery agent app, and submitted to the App Store — all in one week.

4
Launch

City 1 live — then City 2, then City 3

Dukancart launched in their first city and began taking orders within hours. They worked with Applume to replicate the setup for City 2 in 3 days, and City 3 two weeks later.

5
Scale

3-city operation — still zero developers

Today Dukancart operates hyperlocal grocery delivery across 3 cities with the same Applume infrastructure. Their customers have no idea they don't have an in-house tech team.
