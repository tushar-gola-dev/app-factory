The Journey

How it happened, step by step.

1
The Problem

Great idea, no tech team, tight deadline

The founders of Blue Basket knew the market — their city needed a reliable, fast grocery app. But hiring a dev team would take months and ₹40+ lakhs. They needed a faster path.

2
Discovery

30-minute call, product chosen in hours

After a single discovery call with Applume, the team chose the Grocery platform. Within hours they had a product roadmap, brand customisation checklist, and a go-live date: 6 days away.

3
Build

Brand applied, payments live, apps submitted

Applume configured the customer app and delivery partner app with Blue Basket's branding, integrated Razorpay, set up the store admin panel, and submitted to the App Store — all within 5 days.

4
Launch

Live on Day 6 — first order within the hour

Blue Basket went live on iOS and Android on Day 6. Their first order came in within 60 minutes of launch. The Applume infrastructure scaled automatically as order volume grew.

5
Growth

10,000+ orders in 30 days, zero downtime

By the end of month one, Blue Basket had processed over 10,000 orders with 98% platform uptime. They expanded to 3 neighbourhoods without any additional technical investment.
